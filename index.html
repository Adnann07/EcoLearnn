About EcoLearn

EcoLearn is an innovative mobile application designed to provide free education while promoting environmental protection. The app aligns with the 3Zero concept by Prof. Dr. Muhammad Yunus, focusing on zero net carbon emissions, reducing inequality, and ending poverty.

To enroll in a course, users must participate in social work that benefits the environment, such as planting trees, participating in voluntary cleanups, recycling, or using eco-friendly products.

Our platform is designed to be inclusive, with dedicated sections for the visually impaired and specially-abled individuals. We believe that education should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their financial status or physical abilities.
